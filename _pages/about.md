I’m a computer scientist and engineer interested in building systems that make complex ideas easier to understand and use. My work spans theoretical computer science, data systems, and emerging computing paradigms, with a particular focus on quantum computation, quantum information, and machine learning theory.

I’m especially drawn to problems that connect theory with practice: designing clean tools, building reliable infrastructure, and translating abstract concepts into systems that people can reason about and apply. My work includes research, software engineering, analytics architecture, and technical tooling across both academic and operational environments.

This site serves as a collection of my projects, research interests, technical writing, and ongoing work.

Outside of computing, I spend time outdoors, continue learning across disciplines, and make space for family and close friends.”
