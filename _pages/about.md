I am a master's student in Computer Science and Engineering at UC San Diego and a Business Intelligence Analyst at Unified Business Technologies, Inc. My academic interests are in theoretical computer science, with a particular focus on quantum computation, quantum information science, and machine learning theory.

I like work that makes abstract ideas easier to reason about: clean tooling, careful explanations, and small systems that turn messy information into something useful. Outside of work and school, I enjoy exploring, learning, spending time outdoors, and spending time with family and friends.

## portfolio

- [adoctl](https://github.com/cdylpp/adoctl): a Python CLI for validating agent-generated planning bundles and safely creating Azure DevOps work items.
- [Student Retention Analytics Tool](https://github.com/cdylpp/srt): a capstone analytics project focused on student retention modeling, data pipelines, and actionable insights for educational institutions.
- [HomeHub](https://github.com/cdylpp/homehub): a smart home management system concept for organizing shared schedules, tasks, and household workflows.
- [Personal website](https://github.com/cdylpp/cdylpp.github.io): this Jekyll and GitHub Pages site, cleaned up from a general academic template into a focused professional portfolio.

## work history

My current work combines graduate study at UC San Diego with business intelligence work at Unified Business Technologies. Recent project work includes developer tooling for Azure DevOps workflows, student-retention analytics, and small web systems for organizing shared information.
