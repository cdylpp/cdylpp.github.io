Hi! I am a masters' student in the [CSE](https://cse.ucsd.edu) department at [UC San Diego](https://ucsd.edu) with a focus on quantum computation and quantum information. My research interests are motivated by developing full-stack architectures for resource-efficient, fault-tolerant quantum computing systems. My work spans quantum error correction, algorithms and compilation, and hardware architecture.

A collection of projects, research interests, technical writing, and ongoing work is hosted here.

Outside of computing, I enjoy exploring, traveling, reading, and continually learning; anything to keep those ridges from smoothing over.
